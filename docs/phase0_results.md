## Design decisions

Recorded after Phase 0. These set the analysis design for Phase 1 and later.

1. Building change is measured with Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal, 2016 to 2023. GHSL GHS-BUILT-S 2015 and 2020 are used as an independent cross-check. GHSL 2025 is a model projection and is not used as observed data.
2. Vegetation change is measured with Sentinel-2 L2A NDVI composites.
3. ESA WorldCover is used only as a baseline tree mask. It is not used to measure change, because the 2020 (v100) and 2021 (v200) maps were produced with different algorithm versions.
4. Dry-season composites are pooled over two periods: early, December 2016 to February 2018 (dry seasons 2016/17 and 2017/18); late, December 2023 to February 2025 (dry seasons 2023/24 and 2024/25). Pooling two seasons per period compensates for the small number of low-cloud scenes in a single dry season.
5. Surface temperature comes from Landsat 8 only in both periods, so that sensor differences between Landsat 8 and Landsat 9 do not enter the change estimate.
