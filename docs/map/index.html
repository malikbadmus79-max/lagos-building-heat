Is Lagos building itself hotter?

Each square on the map is 500 m across. Its colour shows how much hotter or cooler the ground got than the rest of Lagos, comparing the dry seasons of 2016–18 with 2023–25.

Across 9,157 squares, places where more buildings went up generally warmed more: about 0.6 °C extra for every extra 10% of the land covered by buildings.

329 hotspot squares, among the top fifth for both new building and warming, are home to about 896,000 people; green squares (104) are still mostly green but are being built on fast.

Data years: buildings 2016–2023; heat and greenery: dry seasons 2016–18 vs 2023–25.

Limit: satellite ground temperature, not air temperature; shows patterns, not proof of cause.
